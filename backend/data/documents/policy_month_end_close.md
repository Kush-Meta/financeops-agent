# Month-End Close Policy

1. Bank reconciliations must be completed within 3 business days of month-end.
2. Timing differences under $500 may be classified as timing if cleared within 5 days.
3. Journal entries posted on weekends require dual approval.
4. Vendor invoices above $50,000 require AP manager review.
5. Duplicate invoice numbers or identical amount+vendor+date within 7 days are escalated.
